Legal & Ad Transparency

Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a project brief, request a creator quote, apply as a creator, enroll in a studio class, or book a sponsorship on SonicReel, we collect the contact details and project notes you voluntarily provide (such as your name, email address, portfolio link, and project budget) solely to fulfill your request.

2. Advertising, Cookies & Google AdSense Disclosure

To keep project posting free for clients and starter profiles free for independent creators, SonicReel displays clearly labeled partner sponsorships and may serve third-party advertisements through Google AdSense and similar advertising networks.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (such as the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our visitors based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising at any time by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices.

3. Local Browser Storage

We use standard browser storage (localStorage) to remember custom project briefs and preferences on your device. You can clear your browser storage at any time through your browser settings.

4. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA & GDPR)

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal contact information you have submitted to us by emailing hello@sonicreel.studio. We never sell personal email lists to data brokers.

5. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, ad partnership questions, or data removal requests, email hello@sonicreel.studio.
